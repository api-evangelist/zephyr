The Zephyr community came together at Open Source Summit India 2026 in Mumbai to share knowledge and explore developments, tooling, and real-world applications across embedded systems. In this first post-event blog, we highlight two sessions from the Zephyr track. One explored why Zephyr can initially feel overwhelming to bare-metal developers, while the other examined how downstream Zephyr releases can keep pace with upstream development.
