	<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The meetup brought together developers, engineers, and open source enthusiasts from a variety of backgrounds. Whether experienced in Zephyr or just getting started, participants had the chance to connect, learn, and exchange ideas in an open and collaborative environment.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The evening began with welcome drinks and snacks, followed by an introduction to Zephyr-related activities at Microchip and ongoing efforts to enhance support across their hardware platforms.</span></p>
<h4><strong>Session Highlights:</strong></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The agenda featured a range of sessions focused on practical applications and real-world use cases of Zephyr:</span></p>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zephyr @ Microchip</span></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An overview of current initiatives, including improving hardware support, addressing security and reliability, and contributing to the broader open source ecosystem.</span></p>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zephyr at Scale – Shipping Secure and Maintainable Devices</span></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Insights into building and maintaining multiple globally deployed products using Zephyr, including CI pipelines, device servicing, and managing reusable code across projects.</span></p>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">Smart Motor Control with Zephyr</span></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A practical walkthrough of implementing a motor control application, highlighting development challenges and effective solutions using Zephyr’s modular design.</span></p>

	<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Throughout the sessions, attendees explored:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Real-world Zephyr-based products and deployments</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Development workflows and best practices</span></li>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The talks reflected the growing adoption of Zephyr in production environments and its flexibility across a wide range of use cases.</span></p>
