The Zephyr Project community is heading to Braunschweig, Germany! Join us on Thursday, August 13, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Meetup organized by Siemens , with TU Braunschweig serving as the venue host. STMicroelectronics is supporting the meetup as the hardware board sponsor.
