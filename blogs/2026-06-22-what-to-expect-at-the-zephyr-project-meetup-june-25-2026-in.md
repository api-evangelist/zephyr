The Austin meetup runs 5:00-7:30 PM CT at Analog Devices' office with short talks, hands-on hardware demos, technical discussions, and networking. Speakers include Kate Stewart on Zephyr's growth to 1000+ boards, Brian Cain on Qualcomm's Hexagon architecture port, and Shankar Kalpathi Easwaran on ELD, an open-source embedded linker delivering 100x performance improvements.
