An afternoon workshop (2:00-5:00 PM) covers Zephyr fundamentals with hands-on exercises on Nordic nRF54L15 hardware. An evening meetup follows with talks on build optimization, custom debug adapters, zero-latency interrupts, and integrating custom bootloaders into Zephyr applications.
