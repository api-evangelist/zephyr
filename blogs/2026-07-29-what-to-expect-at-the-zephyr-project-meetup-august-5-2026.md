The Zephyr Project community is heading to Hyderabad, India! Join us on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Meetup organized by Dotstar Systems, with Silicon Labs serving as the venue host. This community gathering will bring together developers, engineers, contributors, technology leaders, students, and embedded systems enthusiasts for an evening of technical presentations, real-world use cases, networking, and discussions around the Zephyr RTOS.
