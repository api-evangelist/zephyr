The Zephyr community is heading to Prague this October for Open Source Summit Europe and Embedded Linux Conference Europe 2026. Taking place from October 7–9 at the Prague Congress Centre, the event brings together open source developers, contributors, maintainers, technologists, community leaders, and end users to exchange knowledge, collaborate on solutions, and advance open source projects. Open Source Summit is a cross-domain gathering for the open source ecosystem.
