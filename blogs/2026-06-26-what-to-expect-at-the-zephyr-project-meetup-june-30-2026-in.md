The Zephyr Project community is heading to Lyon, France! Join us on Tuesday, June 30, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Meetup organized by Witekio . This community gathering will bring together developers, engineers, contributors, technology leaders, and embedded systems enthusiasts for an evening of technical talks, real-world use cases, networking, and discussions around the Zephyr RTOS.
