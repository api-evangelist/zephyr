<div class="wpb_text_column wpb_content_element ">
	<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">From March 10–12, 2026, the global embedded systems community gathered in Nuremberg for the 24th edition of the embedded world Exhibition &amp; Conference 2026.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“This year once again impressively demonstrated the significance that embedded world holds within the global embedded community,” emphasized Benedikt Weyerer, Executive Director of embedded world. “The energy in the halls &#8211; crowded aisles, many fruitful discussions, and an impressive variety of ideas &#8211; truly inspired us. This strong feedback motivates us enormously and reinforces our commitment to continuing on the event’s successful path.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That energy was reflected in the numbers, with around 36,000 visitors from nearly 90 countries attending the event, an increase of over 13 percent compared to 31,783 visitors last year. As the embedded world continues to grow, it is also expanding globally, with embedded world India set to take place in Bengaluru from November 17 to 19, 2026. </span><a href="https://www.elektroniknet.de/international/over-13-percent-more-visitors-than-last-year.5a8d0c33-1466-45b2-8839-0997c16a618b.html"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Read more</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Since its launch in 2003, embedded world has grown into one of the leading global platforms combining a large-scale exhibition with a world-class technical conference focused on applied research and industrial innovation. The event brings together engineers, developers, companies, researchers, and open source communities to explore technologies shaping the future of embedded systems.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This year was especially significant for the Zephyr community, marking the 10-year anniversary of the Zephyr Project, first launched at embedded world in 2016.</span></p>

	<h4><strong>Zephyr Project Booth:</strong></h4>
<p><a href="https://www.zephyrproject.org/what-to-expect-from-the-zephyr-project-at-embedded-world-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Zephyr Project was located</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> in Hall 4, Stand 4-170, where activities took place throughout the exhibition.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Visitors experienced:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Live Zephyr RTOS demos from ecosystem members</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Technical discussions with developers and maintainers</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Opportunities to connect with companies building products with Zephyr</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Special activities celebrating the 10-year anniversary of the Zephyr Project</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ecosystem members showcasing demos at the booth included Antmicro, Nordic Semiconductor, Silicon Labs, NXP Semiconductors, Renesas Electronics, AC6, inovex GmbH, Doulos, Beningo Embedded Group, Alif Semiconductor, STMicroelectronics, Blecon, Microchip Technology, and Percepio.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Many ecosystem companies also showcased Zephyr-powered demos at their own booths across the exhibition hall. To learn more about the demos, please read the </span><a href="https://www.zephyrproject.org/zephyr-rtos-in-action-demos-at-embedded-world-2026/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Zephyr RTOS in Action: Demos at embedded world 2026</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> blog. You can also watch a short glimpse of the demo videos in this </span><a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzRQULb6-ipEwcz5MdZk5-TB_HCrZBAPm&amp;si=696dkMWYHGN0cmuS"><span style="font-weight: 400;">EW26 playlist</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
