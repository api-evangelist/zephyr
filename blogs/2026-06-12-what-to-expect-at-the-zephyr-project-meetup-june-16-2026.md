The Zephyr Project community is heading to Wrocław, Poland! Join us on Tuesday, June 16, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Meetup organized and hosted by the GoodByte team. This meetup is open to anyone interested in the Zephyr ecosystem, open source, embedded systems, and modern embedded software development.
