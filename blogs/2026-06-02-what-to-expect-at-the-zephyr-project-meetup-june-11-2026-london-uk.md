The Zephyr Project community is hosting an in-person meetup in London on June 11, 2026, featuring technical presentations on UART driver development, Zephyr's ten-year evolution, smart wearables, and Bluetooth development. Attendees will have the chance to learn from technical talks and real-world Zephyr use cases while networking with embedded systems professionals and exploring contributions to the project.
