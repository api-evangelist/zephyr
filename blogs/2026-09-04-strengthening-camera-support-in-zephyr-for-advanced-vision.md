The Zephyr community came together at Open Source Summit India 2026 in Mumbai to share knowledge and explore developments, tooling, and real-world applications across embedded systems. In this second post event blog, we recap two lightning talks from the Zephyr track focused on camera support. One explored the architectural changes needed for Zephyr to support AI-driven camera workloads, while the other compared the camera frameworks in Zephyr and Linux and discussed potential enhancements for more advanced vision applications.
