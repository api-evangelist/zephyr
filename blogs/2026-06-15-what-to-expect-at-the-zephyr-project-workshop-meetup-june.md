The Zephyr Project community is heading to Vienna, Austria! Join us on Wednesday, June 17, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Workshop & Meetup hosted by local developers from Vienna. The event will take place at the Expat Center of the Vienna Business Agency, located at Schmerlingplatz 3, 1010 Vienna, Austria.
