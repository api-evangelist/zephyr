The Zephyr Project community is heading to Anaheim, California, for embedded world North America 2026 , taking place September 22–24 at the Anaheim Convention Center, Anaheim, California. Embedded world North America is a leading trade show and conference for innovators and developers across the embedded technology ecosystem. The three-day event will bring together design engineers, systems architects, firmware developers, product leaders, and other embedded professionals to explore the latest technologies and tools shaping embedded innovation.
