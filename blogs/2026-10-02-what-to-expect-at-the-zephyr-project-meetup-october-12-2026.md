The Zephyr Project community is heading to Munich, Germany! Join us on Monday, October 12, 2026, for an in-person Zephyr Project Meetup hosted by Microchip. The event is open to developers, engineers, contributors, students, technology professionals, and anyone interested in open source, embedded systems programming, and software development.
