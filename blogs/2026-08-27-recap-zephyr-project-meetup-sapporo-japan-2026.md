This blog was written by members of the Zephyr community in Japan. The meetup speakers are referred to by their Discord names throughout the blog. On June 26, 2026, the Zephyr Project Meetup returned to Sapporo, Hokkaido, bringing together Zephyr users, embedded developers, hardware engineers, researchers, and open source contributors for an afternoon of technical talks and community discussion.
