Marking 10 years, Zephyr now sees 3.5 commits per hour, with 436 developers pushing over 2,500 commits every month, and supports over 1,000 boards and 300 integrated sensors. Survey data shows 49% of organizations cite easier hardware portability as the primary adoption benefit, while maintenance and long-term support are named the top challenge ahead.
