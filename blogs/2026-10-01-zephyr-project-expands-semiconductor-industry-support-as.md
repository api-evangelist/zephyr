Summary Infineon Technologies and Texas Instruments have upgraded to Platinum membership in the Zephyr Project, deepening their investment in the open source RTOS and its developer community. These increased commitments provide additional resources for Zephyr’s technical development, infrastructure and community programs. The announcement coincides with the Zephyr Developer Summit (October 7–9) and Zephyr Maintainers Forum (October 6) in Prague, where the community marks Zephyr’s 10th anniversary with more than 40 technical sessions, over 45 speakers and the first-ever Zephyr Community Awards.
